About Ascend Abroad

Your global journey starts with the right guidance.

Ascend Abroad is a student-focused initiative designed to help young people explore international education, discover opportunities and build the skills needed to pursue their ambitions.

Through structured learning, practical resources and personalised sessions, students are introduced to different aspects of international education and professional development.

Batch 3 brings together academic exploration, college application preparation and opportunities for personal growth.

What happens in Batch 3?

Six days. New perspectives. New possibilities.

Batch 3 combines four days of scheduled live learning sessions with subsequent personalised sessions.

    Kick-off

    Programme kick-off and first live learning session.

  2. Day 02 | 22 November 2026

    Structured learning

    Second day of structured learning and exploration.

  3. Day 03 | 23 November 2026

    Interactive learning

    Third day of interactive learning and guidance.

  4. Day 04 | 24 November 2026

    Final day of the scheduled core sessions.

  5. Personalisation | 25–26 November 2026

    Aradhya will begin personalised sessions, allowing students to explore their individual goals and next steps.

Important information
  • Core sessions take place every day from 21 to 24 November.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

When does Ascend Abroad Batch 3 begin?

Batch 3 begins on 21 November 2026.

When are the live sessions?

The scheduled core sessions take place from 21 to 24 November, every day at 7:00 PM IST.

How long will each session last?

Each session is expected to last approximately 1–1.5 hours.

What happens after 24 November?

Personalised sessions led by Aradhya will begin from 25 to 26 November.

What is the difference between Accelerator and VIP?

Accelerator costs ₹800 and focuses on college application preparation, including templates, professional email writing and essay writing.

VIP costs ₹1,000 and focuses on first-income opportunities, skill monetisation, professional development and learning from a UNICEF-certified professional.
